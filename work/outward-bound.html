overview

In my final semester, I interned for 16 weeks at Outward Bound Singapore, creating content for the Outdoor Adventure Education (OAE) industry.

  • Developed digital media skills and planned content strategies for the OAE Council.
  • Produced blogs and EDMs, and contributed to the council's podcast plans.
  • Designed collaterals and merchandise, from year-in-review publications to a corporate polo.
  • Collaborated with industry professionals to deliver content aligned with the council's mission.

oae council key highlights 2024

Three directions

A year-in-review publication that showcases the council's achievements and milestones — and a benchmark for progress year on year. I proposed three visual directions for approval.

direction 01

Magazine

A magazine-style, adventurous feel. The soft gradient background gives it a modern, polished look.

Informative yet inviting — setting the tone for a year-in-review that celebrates a dynamic, connected field.

direction 02

Rugged

A rugged, earthy take with a hands-on, outdoorsy feel. Torn-paper edges around the photos keep it informal and true to fieldwork.

The muted green adds an organic touch that complements the theme of exploration.

direction 03

Formal

A formal yet approachable version. Lush greenery conveys openness and a connection with nature.

Even with a more professional tone, the important information stays clear and celebratory.

oae conference 2025

The invitation

EDM routes for the Outdoor Adventure Education Conference on 17 March 2025, which launched the Singapore Standards for Outdoor Adventure Education Activities — from misty mountain illustration to polaroid collages.

council corporate polo

Wear the brand

A branded polo to build identity, unity and professionalism among council members. I proposed clean, striped and diagonal directions — plus vest alternatives suggested by the Secretariat — for members to rank.

council & sswg appreciation lunch

Thank-you notes

An email banner for the appreciation lunch, plus 21 personalised thank-you cards for council and SSWG members.

podcast, vlog & vodcast series

A series for the sector

A proposal for a content series on the OAE Singapore website, featuring stakeholders and their experiences in the industry — to raise awareness, educate and include.

Podcast

Audio (or video) episodes around a theme — storytelling you can take anywhere.

Vlog

Video entries with supporting text and graphics — immersive and accessible.

Vodcast

Audio and video together, adding a visual layer to the conversation.

  1. short term

    Foster connections and raise public awareness of outdoor adventure education and its unique experiences.

  2. mid term

    Build a valuable resource for the sector and a sense of community among people interested in OAE.

  3. long term

    Contribute to the growth of outdoor adventure education in Singapore, shaping lifelong learning.

I also proposed a 2025 photoshoot to build an authentic image library of local OAE activities — from high elements at OBS Pulau Ubin to kayaking and nature trails.
