overview

For my Final Year Project I joined Campus Television (CTV) — Singapore's first campus TV station, by Ngee Ann Polytechnic — as the team's Social Media Manager.

  • Produced content for CTV's Instagram, YouTube and TikTok.
  • Used analytics tools to track and evaluate social media performance.
  • Directed and hosted Campus Meets Career — the biggest project I took on in the capstone.

the alumni feature

Campus meets career

Campus Meets Career investigates the motivational career pathways that graduates of Ngee Ann Polytechnic have chosen. As director and host, I shaped the story and guided our alumni through their journeys on camera.
